Cristian Turetta is a research assistant at the University of Verona and a member of IoT4Care. He received his BSc and MSc degrees in Computer Science and Engineering from the University of Verona in 2017 and 2020. His early research focused on **Active and Assisted Living, Internet of Things systems and IoT security**, and has since developed into a broader programme on wireless and contactless sensing for people and their environment.

His work spans low-cost body-area networks and Bluetooth sensing, WiFi Channel State Information, smart-home platforms and machine-learning methods for **human activity recognition and occupancy sensing**. A recurring objective is to extract useful information from sensing infrastructure that is inexpensive and deployable in ordinary environments rather than requiring specialised motion-capture laboratories.

More recent research investigates contactless physiological and behavioural sensing, including mmWave radar for breathing-rate estimation, WiFi-based sensing and lightweight models for fall detection. Cristian has collaborated extensively with Graziano Pravadelli and Florenc Demrozi on IoT4Care research concerning daily-life assistance, healthcare monitoring and Parkinson's disease, and contributes to the group's transition from wearable sensing toward multimodal and contactless systems.

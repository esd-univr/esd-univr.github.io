Enrico Fraccaroli is a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Fellow at the University of Verona and the
University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill.

His research concerns electronic design automation for mixed-signal and cyber-physical
systems: modelling and injecting faults across the electrical, mechanical and thermal
domains, and building digital twins of production plants.

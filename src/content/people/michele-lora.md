Michele Lora works on the design and verification of embedded and cyber-physical systems,
with a focus on assume-guarantee contracts and on the algorithmic synthesis of control
software for smart manufacturing.

He held a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Global Fellowship for the DeFacto project, jointly at the
University of Southern California and the University of Verona.

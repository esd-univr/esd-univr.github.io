Franco Fummi received the Laurea degree in Electronic Engineering in 1990 and the Ph.D. in
Electronic and Communication Engineering in 1994, both from Politecnico di Milano. Since 2001
he is Full Professor in Computer Architecture at the University of Verona, where he leads the
Electronic Systems Design group.

His research concerns hardware description languages and electronic design automation
methodologies for the modelling, verification, testing and optimisation of embedded systems.

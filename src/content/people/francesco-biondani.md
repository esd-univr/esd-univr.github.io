Francesco Biondani is a PhD student at the Department of Computer Science of the University
of Verona. He obtained his Bachelor's degree in Computer Science in 2020 and his Master's
degree in 2023, both from the University of Verona.

His research focuses on digital twins and Industry 5.0, in particular on predictive
maintenance. His PhD is sponsored by Leonardo S.p.A.

Davide Quaglia is Associate Professor at the Department of Computer Science of the University of Verona. His research concerns methodologies and tools for the design and simulation of networked and distributed embedded systems, with applications including the Internet of Things, cyber-physical systems, smart manufacturing and precision agriculture.

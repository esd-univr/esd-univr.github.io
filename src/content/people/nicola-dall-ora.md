Nicola Dall'Ora is an Assistant Professor at Guglielmo Marconi University. He received his PhD in Computer Science from the University of Verona in 2023, where his work with ESD focused on multi-domain modelling, simulation and fault-based analysis of smart and cyber-physical systems.

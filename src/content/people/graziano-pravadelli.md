Graziano Pravadelli is Full Professor of information processing systems at the Department of
Computer Science of the University of Verona.

His main interests are the semi-formal verification of hardware/software embedded systems and
the design and validation of virtual coaching systems that promote the well-being and health
of people through IoT technologies. He is a co-founder of EDALab, a spin-off developing
embedded software and IoT-based solutions.

Graziano Pravadelli is Full Professor of Information Processing Systems at the Department of Engineering for Innovation Medicine of the University of Verona. His research bridges **embedded-system design and verification** with IoT technologies for health and assisted living, and he is one of the founders of the IoT4Care research line.

On the systems side, his work includes semi-formal and assertion-based verification of hardware/software systems, design methodologies for embedded and cyber-physical platforms, simulation, testing and runtime analysis. He has also worked on reconfigurable systems and design automation, often with the objective of detecting incorrect behaviour early and making complex embedded systems easier to validate.

In IoT4Care, those engineering methods are applied to **human activity recognition, wearable and wireless sensing, virtual coaching and digital health**. Research with his group has addressed daily-life assistance, low-cost body-area networks, BLE and WiFi sensing, contactless mmWave monitoring, fall detection and quantitative analysis of Parkinsonian motor symptoms. The common goal is to turn sensing and embedded intelligence into systems that can operate outside tightly controlled laboratory settings while remaining practical, interpretable and verifiable enough for health-related applications.

Graziano is also a co-founder of EDALab, a University of Verona spin-off working on embedded software and IoT-based solutions.

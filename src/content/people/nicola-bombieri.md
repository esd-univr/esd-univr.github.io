Nicola Bombieri is a professor at the Department of Computer Science of the University of
Verona, where he founded and heads the PARCO Lab.

His research interests include parallel and heterogeneous architectures, edge computing and
parallel programming languages: he develops techniques to customise efficient software for
multi-core, many-core and heterogeneous architectures, targeting performance, power and
energy efficiency. His work also covers electronic design automation applied to the
modelling and verification of smart systems, and parallel computing applied to
bioinformatics and systems biology.

The ICE Laboratory was set up to promote synergy between the technological innovation of
Industry 4.0 and the education and research centers located in the Verona area. The facility
is part of the IT excellence project, which was awarded 8 million euro by the Italian Ministry
of Education, Universities, and Research. The project involves creating prototypes that can be
used in production lines with the integration of augmented reality.

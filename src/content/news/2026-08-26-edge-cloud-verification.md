Nicola Bombieri, Graziano Pravadelli and collaborators published [**Edge-Cloud Orchestration of Assertion-Based Monitors for Robotic Applications**](https://dblp.org/rec/journals/tecs/BombieriGLP26) in **ACM Transactions on Embedded Computing Systems**, volume 25, issue 2 (2026).

The work connects runtime verification with edge-cloud orchestration for robotic systems, bringing together themes from heterogeneous computing and embedded-system verification.

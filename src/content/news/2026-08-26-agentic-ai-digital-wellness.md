At **DATE 2026**, Francesco Biondani, Franco Fummi and collaborators presented [**Agentic AI for Digital Wellness: Challenges and Architectural Perspectives for Smart Home Care**](https://dblp.org/rec/conf/date/CapogrossoBBCPVF26).

The paper examines architectural challenges around agentic AI for smart-home care and digital wellness, extending the group's work on cyber-physical systems toward human-centred applications.

Recent publications from the ESD group cover several stages of the design and validation of cyber-physical production systems.

At **DATE 2025**, Francesco Biondani, Enrico Fraccaroli, Franco Fummi and collaborators presented [**Human-Centered Digital Twin for Industry 5.0**](https://dblp.org/rec/conf/date/BiondaniCDFCF25), focusing on digital-twin technology for human-centred industrial systems.

At **INDIN 2025**, Pietro Turco, Enrico Fraccaroli, Michele Lora, Franco Fummi and collaborators presented [**Frost: A Simulation Platform for Early Validation and Testing of Manufacturing Software**](https://dblp.org/rec/conf/indin/TurcoGFLCF25).

The line of work continued at **VLSID 2026** with [**Machine Dynamics-Aware CPS Scheduling and Iterative Validation for Industry 4.0**](https://dblp.org/rec/conf/vlsid/TurcoFCF26), by Pietro Turco, Enrico Fraccaroli, Samarjit Chakraborty and Franco Fummi.

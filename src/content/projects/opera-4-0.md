OPERA 4.0 developed a privacy-aware industrial monitoring approach aimed at preventing human errors and hazardous conditions in Industry 4.0 workplaces. The project combined formal modelling of work processes with monitoring technologies so that potentially critical behaviours and interactions with machinery could be identified before or during operation.

The technical programme included machine vision, wearable sensing for fatigue or loss of concentration, real-time localisation of people and objects, and formal assessment of the privacy impact of monitoring actions. The resulting methods were intended to be integrated into an Industry 4.0 demonstrator together with industrial partners.

The University of Verona institutional project record gives a starting date of 1 January 2021 and a duration of 24 months, with Davide Quaglia as project manager and Fondazione Cariverona as funder. The current institutional ESD project history includes OPERA 4.0; the former CISD site, however, catalogued the same project under **Networked Systems and Technologies (NeST)**. That historical classification is preserved here rather than silently rewritten.

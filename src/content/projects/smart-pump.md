Smart-Pump aimed to build an intelligent assistive system for regulating continuous drug administration in people with Parkinson’s disease. The system was conceived to monitor motor response and use that information to support more personalised and effective therapy, particularly for people experiencing motor fluctuations or with limited access to specialist visits.

The project also aimed to provide clinicians with a more detailed view of the patient's motor response, supporting assessment of treatment effectiveness and disease evolution. University of Verona and Veneto Region records identify Graziano Pravadelli as scientific lead and project code `1695-0013-1463-2019`.

A public project activity record gives the period as July 2020 to July 2021. Those month-level dates are normalised here to the first and last day of the stated months so the project can use the site’s date schema. The former CISD site recorded Florenc Demrozi as referent and classified Smart-Pump under Internet of Things 4 Care.

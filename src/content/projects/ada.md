ADA investigated how virtual coaching could support ageing people with Down syndrome in maintaining autonomy, social well-being and healthy daily routines. The approach combined unobtrusive smart objects and Internet of Things technologies with data analytics that recognised a person's needs, matched them with a coaching plan and selected a personalised response.

The project used medication-taking as a demonstrator for gathering requirements and assessing the feasibility of the approach. Its wider scope was multidisciplinary, bringing together social-care and coaching expertise, education for people with intellectual disabilities, and ICT research in IoT, embedded systems and data analytics.

The former CISD site recorded Florenc Demrozi as project referent and listed Fabio Chiarani, Stefano Tamburin, Graziano Pravadelli, Florenc Demrozi and Cristian Turetta as contributors. Only current published CISD people are linked in the structured `people` field above.

Bip-Bip investigated wearable sensing and intelligent assistance for people with Parkinson’s disease affected by freezing of gait. The project aimed to recognise conditions associated with an imminent freezing episode and support the person with timely cues, with the broader goal of improving autonomy and quality of life.

Public University of Verona material identifies Graziano Pravadelli as project lead and records research activity by Enrico Fraccaroli during the project period. The former CISD site recorded Florenc Demrozi as referent and classified the project under Internet of Things 4 Care.

The public project activity record gives the period as October 2018 to September 2019. Those month-level dates are normalised here to the first and last day of the stated months so the project can use the site’s date schema without claiming additional project milestones.
